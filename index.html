About

I’m a senior full-stack engineer, AI engineer, and technical team lead. I help startups, agencies, and established teams turn ideas into software: AI-powered applications, modern web products, intelligent automation, and delivery that holds up.

The work runs across React, TypeScript, Node.js, Vue, Python, Ruby on Rails, PostgreSQL, and Supabase, with LLM API integration and AI application development at the center. SaaS, ecommerce, API development, and security engineering sit in the same practice, along with Web3 and smart contracts.

David is based in New Britain, Connecticut. He is a senior software engineer at Syneos Health, an AI engineer at Defined.ai, and the founder of Davuche Media LLC. Earlier roles covered healthcare products at StudyKIK and AI language evaluation at Appen.
